# The manifest

The manifest is a **stage output, not an input**. The discovery module derives it from your seeds and corpus, and every slot carries the same five provenance fields as any other number in the workflow. You ratify or override.

A slot whose `source` field reads assumed, conventional or standard fails validation.

## Derived by D3 and D4

| Slot | Derived from | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| tau | a cut sweep, bounded by cluster saturation above and separation collapse below | D3 |
| decision shape | the action the seeds show practitioners taking, ruled against role floor, grader and yield | D4, before k |
| k | how many candidates the seeds show practitioners committing per round | D4 |
| label source | methods sections, separating measured truth from annotation derived from other predictions | D4 |
| exposure key | the data's own deposition or release metadata | D4 |
| tool inventory | seeds for what exists, local code for what runs, with a readiness census | D4 |
| subject set | availability plus any stated rung the collaboration requires | D4 |
| delta prior | effect sizes the seeds show practitioners acting on | D4 |

The label distinction carries weight. Measured truth from an experimental loop and annotation read off another model's output are different objects, and the second inherits whatever defect its pipeline carries. The record raised this explicitly against a plan claiming seventeen measured tasks.

## Supplied by you at D5

Two facts about your programme, not about the corpus.

| Input | Why the corpus cannot supply it |
|---|---|
| budget | hours or cells you can actually spend |
| cost of the action | the seeds show what others act on, not what you would act on |

`delta` is the delta prior unless your cost of action overrides it. `S`, the survivor target, is budget divided by measured hours per workflow. The record repriced its own cohort twice, at roughly 17 hours and then 37, so measure it rather than estimating.

## Derived later, never supplied

| Name | Stage |
|---|---|
| p_free, p_paid, Q | P5, re-derived after every batch |
| chance | P6, k over candidates per item |
| sigma_d, MDE, N_min | P7, from the free paired variance |

Q is not a slot. The record's end-to-end yield of one in six carries an exact interval from 0.004 to 0.641. Do not derive a queue from a batch whose pessimistic estimate is more than ten times its point estimate. Count more first.

## Per candidate

Declare the **decision shape**, one of infer, explain, generate or intervene, with its subtype. `references/shape.md` holds the roots, the three gates and the downstream table. Each root defines its own independent unit, its own mechanical floor and its own chance baseline, so a candidate ruled under the wrong root is ruled under the wrong counts. The record closed at least one design candidate under the prediction rule, and that closure may have been wrong.

## Tool roles

| Role | Question | Input from |
|---|---|---|
| encoder | what is this most like | raw data |
| predictor | what does this do | raw data |
| scorer | how plausible is this | a hypothesis the agent wrote |
| generator | what would it look like if | a condition the agent set |
| simulator | what happens when | an intervention the agent chose |

The line falls after predictor. Below it the agent constructs the input, which is where orchestration becomes reasoning. A task needing only the first two roles needs no agent.

## Checks at ratification

- At least one classical counterpart per foundation-model channel, or the comparator cannot attempt every stage.
- At least one role below the encoder and predictor line. An infer candidate sits on that line by construction and needs a recorded lift.
- Every candidate's shape record names a binding gate for each root it rejected.
- Every slot's falsifier names an observation, not a sentiment.
- If every eligible item predates every subject, contamination is disclosed rather than solved.
