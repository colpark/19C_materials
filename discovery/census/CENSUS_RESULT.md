## Headline

- **4 of 30 papers publish a rejected structural model with a number attached.** One of the four is weak. 6 of the 11 numbered rejections come from one XRD-methods paper (Dara, PMC12895389).
- **2 of 30 papers carry both a numbered negative and deposited raw data.**

## What this decides

- **The guideline's premise does not hold in this sample.** It said EXAFS/Rietveld SI tables publish rejected models with R-factors "by convention". Rejections are *named* at 49 of 87 visible branch points (56%). They carry a number at 11 (13%). Every agreement index printed in set Z belongs to the accepted model.
- **C3 (structure solution with found negatives) is supply-bound.** Found negatives exist in 4 of 30 papers. The re-fittable raw data an explain/inverse task needs exists in 2 of 30. The route is **constructed negatives**, disclosed, or a replayable environment.
- **Three protocol problems are recorded, not fixed.**
  1. The frozen queries hit off-target papers: set Z 5 of 8, set E 6 of 10.
  2. Numbers sit inside SI images (EMF/PNG), so text-only mining would have scored 0.
  3. The counting unit was ambiguous (decision point vs model). Decision point was used.

  These bound the census's precision. They do not reverse its direction.
